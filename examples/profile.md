## My recurring traps

- **guard-addition — Overengineering by addition.** Separate small incidents each became a new permanent control layer.
  evidence: example/session-a:12; example/session-b:8 (fictional)
- **research-loop — Research instead of a decision.** Another search postponed the selected next step without a question that could change it.
  evidence: example/session-c:20; example/session-d:14 (fictional)
- **open-loops — Starting before finishing.** A new workstream opened while the current one still had no completion criterion.
  evidence: example/session-e:7; example/session-f:11 (fictional)
